IK-driven Character Body Control System

IK-driven Character Body Control System is an experimental FPP/TPP character interaction system designed to bridge the gap between traditional input devices (keyboard/mouse and gamepad) and 6DoF VR motion controllers. It provides intuitive 3D spatial reach, depth, and orientation control for both hands, while procedural full-body inverse kinematics (VRIK) automatically coordinates the upper body, spine, and posture for natural interaction.

Inspirations & Design Philosophy#

In conventional flat-screen games, environmental interactions are predominantly binary: players press an interact key to trigger canned animations, or manipulate physics props via a crosshair raycast. In contrast, 6DoF VR setups offer direct, tangible physical agency—allowing players to physically reach out, inspect, touch, and slide objects in 3D space.

This project explores how to bring that level of spatial agency to standard peripherals without turning the controls into a chore. The resulting design is heavily shaped by the tactile, manual door manipulation in OPERATOR, the stark contrast in bodily presence between desktop and VR users in VRChat, the decoupled head look (Free Look) of Arma Reforger, and the expressive, physics-aware limb mechanics found in titles like TABG and How to Fish.

Rather than treating character limbs as clumsy ragdoll puppets, the system maintains a strict separation between active player agency and passive procedural compensation:

  • Active Agency (Player Input): The player directly commands spatial intent—toggling either hand, steering reach depth via the scroll wheel, rotating palm pitch/inward roll, or holding pinch-drags.
  • Passive Compensation (Procedural IK): The character's spine curvature, torso twisting, shoulder projection, and elbow hints are procedurally resolved in real-time by a full-body IK solver to preserve natural anatomy.

System Architecture#

The architecture decouples pure spatial mathematics and state logic from Unity engine dependencies and animation rigs.

1. Pure State Machine & Intent Pipeline#

At the foundational layer, HandStateController manages dual independent hand states (Resting, FreeReaching, Retracting, AttachedOpen, and AttachedPinched):

  • Independent Toggles with Shared Depth: Left and right hands can be deployed or retracted independently. When both hands reach out simultaneously, their reach depth smoothly synchronizes.
  • Surface & Handle Attachment (SliderHandleInteractionTarget): Directly inspired by tactile door manipulation in titles like OPERATOR, reaching near interactive props allows hands to snap onto authored contact anchors. Holding pinch/drag inputs routes view pitch or wheel deltas directly into the target's physical travel axes (such as pushing a door handle or sliding a latch) with distinct open, pinch, and release palm poses.

2. Analytical Spatial Reach Solver#

HandReachSolver is a pure C# math solver responsible for calculating the target palm pose before any bone transforms are touched:

  • Converging Reach Path: Reaching outward does not simply cast forward along parallel tracks. The solver linearly interpolates from a broader shoulder spread at close range to a converging spread at maximum extension, mirroring natural human arm kinematics.
  • Orientation Framing: Palm rotation is computed from signed pitch and mirrored inward roll angles relative to a stabilized neutral frame, ensuring predictable wrist postures at any reach height.

3. VRIK Driving & Posture Shaping#

Bridging abstract hand targets into RootMotion's VRIK is handled by HandPoseDriver, which hooks directly into the pre- and post-solve IK passes:

  • Torso Intent & Chest Goal: To avoid unnatural hyperextension when reaching forward or across the body, the driver computes a torso yaw intent clamped within anatomical boundaries, steering the VRIK chest goal to naturally turn the upper body into the reach.
  • Dynamic Elbow Hints: Real-time bend goals are derived from shoulder positions and reach extension. Elbows drop and flare outward predictably depending on reach depth and posture blend weights.
  • Finger Posture Overlays: Hand finger poses are procedurally sampled and overlaid on top of the base animation clips during interaction and release phases.

4. Decoupled Free Look & True First-Person#

Immersion in first-person games often breaks down when looking around forcibly drags the arms with the camera:

  • Free-Look Reach Decoupling: Players can freely swivel their view while freezing the reach frame in the character's own space. Hands keep moving with the body and stay steadily focused on their work plane or attached targets even as the head turns away.
  • True First-Person Mesh Culling: Using FirstPersonPartCuller, the character avatar's head and facial geometry are suppressed for the first-person camera while preserving the torso, arms, legs, and full shadow-casting onto the ground (ShadowsOnly).

Key Takeaways & Current State#

This prototype demonstrates that spatial manual interaction can feel responsive, tactile, and natural on a standard keyboard and mouse when procedural IK takes care of full-body anatomical plausibility. It transforms passive interaction into an expressive, physical mechanic. Gamepad bindings and controls have not been designed yet.
